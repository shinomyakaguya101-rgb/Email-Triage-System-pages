Email Triage System is Jonathan's personal Python automation for organizing authorized Gmail messages, summarizing what matters, and suggesting next actions.

Read the privacy policy ↗

Personal use only. Public account sign-up is not available.

What it does

From message to next step.

01

Read & organize

Reads messages from the Gmail account Jonathan authorizes. Gemini classifies messages, creates summaries, and suggests next actions. Depending on the route, the system applies labels or moves selected low-value promotional mail to Trash.

02

Record & alert

Records triage results in the Email_Triage_Logs tab of one Google spreadsheet. Selected urgent messages generate alerts in a chosen Slack channel.

03

Evaluate job opportunities

Fetches linked public job pages, evaluates opportunities against a private job-preference profile, and records results in the Job_Opportunities tab of the same spreadsheet.

Cold outreach: after classification, this route takes no Gmail, Sheets, or Slack action. The email is still read and sent to the configured AI provider for classification; local completion state may record that it was handled.

Access & operation

Permission has a purpose.

Gmail access supports reading authorized messages, detecting mailbox changes, applying labels, and moving selected messages to Trash. Sheets access supports recording results in the selected spreadsheet.

Mailbox access is authorized through Google OAuth and can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically delete earlier spreadsheet rows, Slack alerts, or processing records.

Cloud processing using Gmail push notifications, Pub/Sub, and a private Cloud Run service has been prepared. Activation and operation while the laptop is off remain unverified.
